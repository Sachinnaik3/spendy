Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you create an account and use Spendly, such as your name, email address and the expenses you record (amount, category, date and description).

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the service, show you your spending history and summaries, keep your account secure, and respond to your requests. We do not use your expense data for advertising and do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage

Your data is stored so that you can access it when you sign in. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Third Party Services

Spendly loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads. We may also use service providers to run Spendly. They may access your data only to help us operate the service and are bound to protect it.

Contact Us

To ask about this policy, or to request access to or deletion of your data, contact the Spendly team using the details provided on the Spendly website.
